See the early universe like NEVER before: Incredible simulation reveals exactly what happened in the first two billion years of our history

An incredible simulation has revealed the earliest moments of our universe as they have never been seen before. This groundbreaking model, dubbed 'Lumina', captures exactly what happened in the first two billion years of our history following the Big Bang. Lumina traces the bafflingly complex interactions between matter, dark matter and radiation, revealing how the very first galaxies formed and shaped the universe around them. Now, stunning images and videos have revealed what that would have looked like at a level of detail never seen by scientists before.
